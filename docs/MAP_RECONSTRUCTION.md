# Map reconstruction — Milestone 9

Version 0.10.0 deliberately favors stable, readable territories over aggressive procedural distortion.

## Geometry

- 23 spaces per kingdom and four Frontier spaces
- Shared curved boundaries with no line crossings
- Wider special-location spaces
- Subtle irregularity without tiny slivers
- A lightly scalloped outer edge
- Placement based on the supplied board photographs

## Label adjustments

Every reported Bazaar, Ruin, Tomb, and Sanctuary problem area has a kingdom-specific position, diagonal angle, maximum width, and font size. All four Dark Tower labels appear on two lines.

## Heraldry

The Citadel shields sit farther outside the board. Their Lion, Winged Lion, Double Eagle, and Unicorn are newly drawn vector artwork inspired by the original heraldic identities.
